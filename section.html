Kimmix, an uncommon Protogen and a high-ranked Nehixim special officer, his purpose is to unravel the mystery of Arcai. Armed with cutting-edge technology and backed by limitless funding from Nehixim, he operates in the shadows, unbound by conscience or consequence. As long as his objective is met, he pays little mind to the obstacles in his way.

While not designed for combat and lacking in physical strength, making direct confrontation a last resort. Instead, he seeks to weaponize Arcai itself—manifesting its power into lethal projectiles. His relentless pursuit of discovery often treads the line between brilliance and ethical transgression. He does not crave destruction, but he won't hesitate to break the rules—or anyone who stands in his way. To him, ideals like justice and honor are nothing more than distractions; only the truth matters.
