KMMX or Kimmix. A researcher, a lone traveler, and self-independence. While was born on EI, he chooses to leave his birth home and travel on a lone journey to learn more about Arcai. Fear of betrayal, he never trusts people easily but wanted to share his knowledge. He chooses to leave behind a copy of the log on his findings for others to find. Usually shy and avoids people, but is very observant from a distance. Upon researching Arcai technology study he can use Arcai power to create force matter materialization which helps to aid his travel journey.
