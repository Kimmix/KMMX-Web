Pre-Nehixim Period

Before official record-keeping began, Kimmix conducted independent research into anomalous energy patterns, developing rudimentary theories that would later align with Arcai principles.

A catastrophic experimental failure resulted in permanent neural alterations and the first instance of involuntary Arcai manifestation, leaving Kimmix with the characteristic rune patterns and an irreversible connection to Arcai energy fields.

Independent Researcher

Nehixim Recruitment

Kimmix's independent research into anomalous energy signatures attracted Nehixim's attention. After monitoring their activities for several months, Nehixim approached with an offer.

Resources and infrastructure in exchange for exclusive research rights. Desperate for proper equipment and theoretical frameworks, Kimmix accepted, though privately harboring reservations about Nehixim's ultimate intentions for Arcai applications.

IDENTITY:
Nehixim Recruit

First Arcai Breakthrough

Successfully documented the crystallization process of Arcai energy, creating the first stable Resonant Shards under laboratory conditions.

This breakthrough established Kimmix as a valuable asset within the Research Division and secured additional funding and autonomy for their projects. During this period, Kimmix began systematically creating duplicate research documentation—the official versions for Nehixim databases and the shadow versions designed for eventual public dissemination.

Development of Neural Interface

Created specialized Neural Dampeners to manage the increasing neural feedback from advanced Arcai manipulation.

This self-preservation project revealed new insights into the biological interface between consciousness and Arcai energy, leading to more precise manipulation techniques and eventually the theoretical framework for what would become the Dimensional Shift ability.
